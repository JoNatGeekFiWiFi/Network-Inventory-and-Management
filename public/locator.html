Fiber Fault Locator

Load a KML or KMZ, enter what the OTDR read, and get coordinates to dig at. Your file is measured and then discarded — its contents are not kept. A record of the upload (file name, size, checksum and network address) is retained for administration.

1 · Route file

Drop a KML or KMZ here or click to choose · also reads GeoJSON, GPX and CSV · up to 8 MB

2 · Fault distances

One row per event on the trace. You can paste several numbers into a box — “1.2, 4.5, 7.8” — and they'll split into rows.
Fibre runs longer than the route because of slack loops and coils, so the entered distance is reduced before it's measured along the ground.
